# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who delegate work to AI coding agents (Claude Code and similar) and need to stay in control of what those agents produce. They work at a desk with a terminal and an agent session open next to Otea, and they also check in from a phone on the couch or away from the desk to see what is waiting for them. They come to Otea in two modes of attention:

- **Triage** (breadth): scan many findings at once, filter, group, prioritize, discard, decide what gets worked on.
- **Review** (depth): inspect one result with its evidence (GIF, video, diff, log), answer concrete questions, approve or send it back.

## Product Purpose

Otea is the asynchronous handoff surface between a human and their AI agents. Agents leave findings, deliverables and evidence; the human leaves decisions and feedback; both can always see where each thing stands and whose turn it is. It is local-first, open source, and shaped by presets that carry both the board and the agent's playbook.

Success: the human always knows at a glance what is waiting for them, decides quickly and confidently, and enjoys the moment of reviewing an agent's work instead of dreading a ticket queue.

## Brand Personality

**Serene, precise, alive.**

- *Serene*: a triage of 200 items never overwhelms; calm hierarchy, generous rhythm.
- *Precise*: everything aligned, every pixel has a reason; components snap together into screens and stay in sync.
- *Alive*: motion tells you what changed. An item that changes turn travels, it does not just reappear.

The identity comes from the name: *otear* means to scan from a high vantage point, to watch what is happening and what is coming. The tone is a calm, attentive lookout, never a nagging ticket system.

## Anti-references

- Jira and grey, dense ticket managers: dull, bureaucratic, joyless.
- The "made by AI" look: purple/violet gradients, identical card grids, glassmorphism, cream/sand backgrounds, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section, hero metrics.
- Generic SaaS dashboards that could belong to any product.

## Design Principles

1. **Whose turn it is comes first.** Turn is the most important signal on every screen and is readable before anything else.
2. **Evidence is the protagonist in review.** The interface steps back so the GIF, video or diff can be judged.
3. **A system, not screens.** Modules share grid, rhythm and motion, so a component contributed by a preset looks native; everything composes and stays in sync.
4. **Motion means something.** Transitions and effects explain what happened (moved, arrived, changed hands); they never decorate.
5. **A pleasure to use.** Care in the details is part of the function, not polish added later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.
- Turn and state are never conveyed by color alone; always paired with shape or text.
- Comfortable touch targets on phone (the UI is used over LAN from a phone).
